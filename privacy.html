Privacy Policy

Outback Gas Station Simulator (Android) · Effective 24 November 2026

In short

  • We don't ask for your name or e-mail address, there's no account, and your save game stays on your device.
  • The game shows video ads only when you choose to watch one for a reward. Google AdMob delivers them and processes device data to do so. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent first, and you can change it at any time under Settings → Privacy → Ad choices.
  • Purchases are handled by Google Play. We never see your payment details.

Data stored only on your device

Your progress (money, upgrades, station level, unlocked cosmetics) and settings (language, graphics, volume) are saved locally on your device. They are not sent to us, and they are deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its app data.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game offers optional rewarded videos, for example an instant fuel delivery or a bonus at the end of the day. Nothing in the game requires you to watch an ad. The ads are delivered by Google AdMob, provided by Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland, and Google LLC (USA).

To show, measure and protect ads, AdMob processes information such as:

  • your device's advertising ID (you can reset or delete it in your Android settings),
  • IP address and an approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version),
  • how you interact with ads (views, taps, completed videos) and diagnostic data.

Consent. If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the game shows Google's consent form (Google User Messaging Platform) before any ad is requested. Personalised ads and storing or reading information on your device are only used with your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and § 25 TDDDG). If you decline, Google may still show non-personalised or limited ads and use some data to prevent fraud and abuse, based on legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). You can change or withdraw your consent at any time under Settings → Privacy → Ad choices, in the tablet in the game or on the title screen.

More about how Google uses data: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

In-app purchases (Google Play)

Optional cosmetic items can be bought with real money. The payment is processed entirely by Google Play under Google's privacy policy. We never receive your name, address or payment details. The game only receives a purchase confirmation (product, order ID and purchase token) so it can unlock the item and restore it later. The game uses Unity Technologies' in-app purchasing library to talk to Google Play, and that library may send technical data about the purchase process to Unity. Legal basis: performance of the contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

The game engine (Unity)

The game is made with the Unity engine by Unity Technologies (San Francisco, USA). Unity may collect limited technical information, such as device model, operating system version and crash or performance data, to keep the engine working reliably. See the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.

How long data is kept

Data on your device stays until you uninstall the game or clear its data. For data processed by Google and Unity, their own retention periods apply, as described in their privacy policies.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data, contact us and we will help remove it.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and to object to processing, and the right to withdraw consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of processing before the withdrawal. Because we hold no personal data ourselves, most requests concern Google or Unity. We will forward or help with them. You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, for example the one where you live.

Changes

We may update this policy when the game changes, for example if new services are added. The current version is always available at this address, and the effective date at the top shows when it last changed.
